OSWM accessible routing

This experimental tool uses mapped OpenStreetMap accessibility information and estimated terrain slope.

  1. Choose Route or Reachability.
  2. Select a routing profile.
  3. For a route, click an origin and destination.
  4. For reachability, choose a starting time, increment and number of increments, then click one origin.
  5. Export calculated reachability polygons as GeoJSON when needed.

Accessibility-adjusted times and polygons are approximate. Missing data can affect the result, and provisional profiles do not guarantee accessibility.
